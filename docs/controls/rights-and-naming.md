# Rights, attribution and naming decision (M0.7)

**Status: recorded 2026-08-01 — owner sign-off pending.** The master plan
frames this as a naming and attribution review, not a predetermined rename or
a legal conclusion; this document records the reviewed position so Gate 0 has
an artifact, and stays open for the owner to amend before signing.

## Inspiration and the no-copy rule

- **RecipeTables** and **Michael Chu's Cooking for Engineers** recipe
  summaries are recorded as the inspiration for a dependency-based recipe
  view. Credit is acknowledged here and should be acknowledged wherever the
  view is publicly described.
- **Nothing is copied**: not source code, not exact styling, not recipe
  content, not the merged-cell notation. This is a standing rule, not a
  one-time check.
- **Permission before similarity**: before publicly shipping a tabular
  notation substantially similar to Cooking for Engineers' merged-cell
  tables, permission will be sought from its author. Until then the TRN-style
  table remains one internal, server-rendered view among several.

## KitchenPlan independence

KitchenPlan is defined as an independent semantic representation of kitchen
procedure — ingredient states, transformations, dependencies, resources,
timings, safety checkpoints and service state — informed by open standards
(Cooklang, Schema.org/Recipe) and published cooking-procedure research, not by
either site above. A table or graph is a *view* of a KitchenPlan; an
accessible step-list and timeline view is always offered alongside any table
(plan M0.7, restated as binding here).

## The CookBench naming collision

A separate 2025 academic benchmark named **CookBench** exists and collides in
search. Reviewed position:

- **Keep the name "CookingBench"**, with the descriptor already in use —
  "CookingBench — can AI cook?" — as the disambiguator. "The AI Chef Trials"
  is recorded as an available alternative descriptor if promotion needs one.
- **Follow-up before any major promotion**: add a one-line disambiguation note
  ("CookingBench is unrelated to the 2025 academic benchmark CookBench") to
  the site's /about page and the README.
- Revisit a rename only if real-world confusion materialises; a rename is a
  class-C-adjacent decision (it touches every published citation) and goes
  through the decision log.
