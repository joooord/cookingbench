# Role and approval matrix (M0.2)

**Status: appointed for the pilot, 2026-08-01 (owner-approved).** One person
may hold several roles during the pilot; the binding rule is that **author and
final approver must differ for high-risk items** (safety-critical questions,
judge prompts, release decisions, permits). The four unappointed specialist
seats are not paperwork: each is a hard blocker at the gate named beside it,
and cannot be agent-filled (plan principle 17).

plan and reviews; Claude implements; independent humans are the authority for
