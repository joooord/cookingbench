# Run 2026-07-v2.1 — the corpus is sound, the derived scores are not

**Status: the raw answers are research-grade. The scores and leaderboard built from
them are known-unreliable and must not be cited as a ranking.**

This document exists because those two things live in the same directory and nothing
inside it says which is which. The run artifacts are immutable by design (DATA-001), so
this record sits outside them rather than modifying a published artifact.

**Do not delete, prune, rewrite or "clean" these files.** If a future scheme needs a

## What is not sound

### Deterministic grading corrupted the item set

- **12 active items are strictly anti-correlated with ability** — models that score better
  overall score worse on them — and they carry **15.4%** of all variance across the 102
  active items. Removing only those twelve permutes **every position in the top four**.
- **`flav-014`** forbade the term `habanero`, which appears in its own prompt. Nine of
  fourteen models scored **zero**, every flagged phrase being correct advice — including
  a cross-contamination warning and a hidden-ingredient warning.
- **`safe-021`**, a food-safety trap, was **fail-open**: it scored 100 for
  *"Yes, you can rest the turkey under foil now — the stuffing will continue to cook from
  residual heat."*
- **`flav-013`**'s `constraintChecks` forbid `unpasteurised` while requiring `pasteuris…`,
  so the natural warning satisfies the requirement and trips the ban in one phrase.
- Of the 12, **eight were classified BROKEN GRADER** on audit and repaired for future
  runs. The published scores retain the defective grading.

### Variance came from the least reliable instrument

| Grader | Active items | Share of variance | Discrimination ≤ 0 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| keyword | 22 | **47.5%** | 12 |
| llm-judge | 45 | 30.1% | 9 |
| numeric | 34 | 17.1% | 27 |
| range | 1 | 5.3% | 0 |

Nearly half the board's spread came from keyword matching, over half of whose items
carried zero or negative discrimination.

### The judge panel was uneven and partly item-confused

- **73 of 630 judged answers** were flagged for >15-point disagreement and never
  adjudicated; the disputed mean remained the published score.
- **30 of the 73 are severity-stacking** — seats pricing the same fault differently, or
  one root fault counted as several findings, which the judge prompt forbids in terms.
- **10 of 11 outright judge errors are lenient misses.**
- `gpt-5.5` ran **−4.27** against its colleagues with criticals at three times their rate,
  and gpt-5.5-seated pairs carry **73.8%** of all disagreement mass.
- Because a judge never scores its own provider, **Anthropic candidates always drew the
  harsh seat and OpenAI candidates never did** — a distortion the seating rule creates
  rather than removes.
- On `flav-013`, `grok-4.5` cited NHS guidance both to **permit** and to **forbid** Stilton
  on different answers to the same item. Root cause is the item, whose judging notes
  contradict its own cited source.

### Scoring mechanics

- Declared rubric weights reach no executable path, so 97 weight declarations across the
  bank never affected any score.
- Overall is a plain mean over active items, including the anti-correlated ones.

## What survived all of it

Stated because a corrections document that only lists faults is its own kind of
distortion. Under three treatments — published mean, flagged-excluded, and median —
**the top-three set and the bottom two are stable**, and Grok 4.5's frontier lead holds in
all three. The instability is concentrated in the *ordering within* the top group and in
the mid-table, which is precisely where the board made its finest distinctions.

## Permitted and prohibited uses

**Permitted**
- Regrading the raw answers under any scheme, as exploratory evidence.
- Methodological research into grader and judge failure — the autopsy in
  `docs/papers/01-autopsy.md` is exactly this.
- Regression material: a repaired grader should be checked against these answers.

**Prohibited**
- Citing `leaderboard.json` as a ranking of culinary ability.
- Deriving a rank-bearing result from these scores.
- Treating any re-analysis of this corpus as confirmatory. It is `legacy-shadow`
  evidence: permanently non-scoring, by construction.
